Calibration

base · shoulder · elbow · wrist

If the arm moves differently from the 3D view, calibrate each joint: 1. move its slider until the joint is at the reference posture and press Reference here. 2. stand on the arm's right side (arm reaching to your right) and move the slider to a bigger angle: shoulder, elbow and wrist must turn up, the base must turn to the arm's left. If not, press Reverse. 3. press Save to arm.

How to use
  1. Switch the arm on, then tap the badge at the top (Demo · tap to connect) and choose RobotArm in the Bluetooth list. The badge turns green.
  2. Press Home once. The servos switch on one by one and the arm goes to its home pose.
  3. Type X, Y, Z in cm. The origin is on the table under the base axis: X points forward, Y to the left, Z up. Leave pitch empty to let the arm choose, or type −90 for a gripper pointing straight down.
  4. The card turns green when the point can be reached. The grey arm is where it is now; the orange arm is where it will go.
  5. Press Preview to watch the motion, then Move arm.
  6. Pick & place: type where the object is (and where to put it), check the step list, press Preview, then run it. Set the gripper's open and closed angles there first.
  7. The Joints tab moves one servo at a time and holds the calibration.
